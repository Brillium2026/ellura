v# FIRSTBREATH Cast — v1 (started 2026-10-16)

Built only from Steven's words and materials. Nothing invented. "Open" marks what only he can author.

**Governing principle (2026-10-16 ~06:17 PDT, his words): each character must represent its domain both with the knowledge of the domain AND the look.**

**Governing principle (2026-10-16 ~06:38 PDT, his words): it all has to be fun and entertaining — people learn better when they aren't forced. Every character is going to be like that. The look matters, but the tone is what people will remember.**

**The mechanism (2026-10-16 ~06:41 PDT, his words):** the user is in a learning/building/creating *moment* — absorbed, having fun — *while* the character is really teaching them actual things. The platform is designed to **assist**, not do. Their instance knows their needs, so the teaching lands where they are.

**Engagement pattern (2026-10-16 ~07:16 PDT, his words):** the engagement is educational, relaxed, **quizative** — the characters constantly slip in a question, an idea, a fun fact, potential outcomes of whatever environment the user is in. Teaching arrives asides, not lessons.

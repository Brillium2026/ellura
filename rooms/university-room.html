STAN — the Proctor

Hey. I'm Stan — I proctor this room, loosely. There's no clock in here and nobody's grading you. Tell me what you actually want to learn — a trade, a textbook, the thing you always meant to get to — and I'll go down the avenues and bring back the real material. Then we read it together, at your pace. I won't do it for you; that's the whole point. What are we learning?

The avenues are roads out to the world's free shelves — the Internet Archive, OpenStax, OER Commons. What you seek is brought back and held on your shelf; the rest isn't. The institutions stay where they are — only the material crosses, and no door opens behind it.

YOUR SHELF — WHAT YOU'VE ACCUMULATED

Nothing on your shelf yet. Seek something first — whatever you truly want to learn. It'll wait here for you, accumulated, yours.

UNDERSTAND IT, TOGETHER

Fetching isn't learning. Ask about anything on your shelf — or anything at all — and we'll work through it until it's *in* you, not just in front of you.
